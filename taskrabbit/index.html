PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL

Book a Tasker from a text message

Homeowners text Dwellable when something in their house needs attention. When the job fits a Tasker, Dwellable books it through TaskRabbit.

Diagnose
Dwellable identifies the job and matches it to the right TaskRabbit category.
Quote and schedule
Estimates and open time slots appear right in the conversation.
Confirm and book
Nothing is booked until the homeowner says yes. Payment stays with TaskRabbit.
Remember
Completed jobs are saved to the home’s maintenance record.
