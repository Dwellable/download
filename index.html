What is Dwellable?

Dwellable is your personal assistant for homeownership. It knows your home, so you can ask it anything by text or in the app, from a stain on the ceiling to when to replace the water heater. It also sends reminders made for your house and finds a local pro when you need one.

How do I add my home?

Enter your number above or text Dwellable, and we'll text you a secure link that's just for you. Open it, sign in with Apple or your email, and add your address. It takes about a minute. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes, so no one else can use it. If you use the app, you add your home when you sign up.

How does Dwellable know about my home?

When you add your home, Dwellable pulls its public records, like the year it was built, its size, and its heating and cooling. It uses them to plan reminders and to answer questions for your house, not a generic one.

Do I need to download an app?

No. You can start by text. The free iOS and Android apps add your property info, reminders and checklists in one place.

Is Dwellable free?

Yes, it's free to start, by text or in the app.

Can Dwellable find someone to fix it?

Yes. When a job needs a pro, Dwellable suggests well-reviewed local pros near you. You choose who to contact.

How can I stop the texts?

Reply STOP at any time and the texts stop. Reply HELP for help. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.
